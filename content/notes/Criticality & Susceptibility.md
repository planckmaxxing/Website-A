---
title: Criticality & Susceptibility
---

In statistical mechanics, a system poised near a continuous phase transition exhibits a diverging susceptibility $\chi$, meaning that infinitesimal local perturbations can trigger macroscopic reorganization alongside [[Scale-Free Correlations|scale-free correlations]].

$$\chi = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i,j} \langle \delta \mathbf{v}_i \cdot \delta \mathbf{v}_j \rangle$$

Biological flocks appear to operate close to this critical boundary: ordered enough to preserve coherent [[Emergent Order Without Control|emergent order]], yet supple enough that a single bird's evasive maneuver on the periphery rapidly recruits the entire ensemble without descending into incoherent noise.
