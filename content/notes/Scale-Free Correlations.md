---
title: Scale-Free Correlations
order: 2
---

Defining the velocity fluctuation of bird $i$ as $\delta \mathbf{v}_i = \mathbf{v}_i - \frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^N \mathbf{v}_k$, the connected spatial correlation function $C(r)$ exhibits no intrinsic decay scale other than the linear extent $L$ of the flock itself, sustained by [[Topological Neighborhoods|topological coupling]].

$$
\xi \propto L, \qquad \omega(k) \approx c_s \, k
$$

Because the correlation length $\xi$ scales linearly with $L$, orientational perturbations do not attenuate exponentially with distance. Instead, directional changes propagate across the swarm as minimally damped, linearly dispersing spin waves with sound speed $c_s$, a hallmark of [[Criticality & Susceptibility|criticality]].
