---
title: Minimal Interfaces & Optical Calm
---

Most digital reading environments compete for attention with chrome, elevation shadows, and saturated accents. A matte dark surface paired with restrained typographic hierarchy removes visual friction, letting prose and motion occupy the same quiet plane, as explored on [[index|About Me]].

Keeping line measures near 60 characters, maintaining consistent 12pt proportions, and stripping away decorative containers allows the eye to move effortlessly between reading and notes such as [[Field Notes 2025]] without cognitive reset.
